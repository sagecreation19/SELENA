Shipping Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

ReturnsRefundsCancellationFAQsContact Support

A. Order Processing

Orders are processed after order confirmation and payment confirmation, where payment is applicable. Processing time is separate from the courier delivery estimate. We carry out a product quality check before dispatch. Any material processing delays will be communicated to customers.

B. Shipping Charges

Shipping charges may vary by destination, order value or parcel type. We will clearly state whether standard shipping is free or chargeable. Express delivery availability and any additional charge will be disclosed. All applicable shipping charges are shown before the customer confirms the order.

C. Estimated Delivery & Tracking

Return Policy — 48-Hour Reporting & Video Evidence

Last Updated: November 2026

ShippingReplacementExchangeRefundsContact Support
Mandatory Requirement: Customers should report eligible delivery issues within 48 hours of the recorded delivery date/time. A continuous unboxing video is the standard required evidence for damage, defect or incorrect-item claims. Photographs alone are not a substitute for the video. This process does not remove any remedy required by applicable consumer law.

A. Eligible Issue Categories

  • Damaged product received
  • Defective product received
  • Incorrect product delivered
  • Missing product from a confirmed order
  • Verified product-quality issue
  • Product materially different from its advertised description

B. Mandatory Unboxing Video

Photographs alone will not be accepted as a substitute for the mandatory unboxing video. The customer should provide a clear, continuous and unedited video showing:

  • The original sealed parcel and shipping label
  • The condition of the parcel before opening
  • The opening of the parcel
  • All contents as removed from the package
  • The product condition immediately after opening
  • Any visible damage, defect or incorrect item

The original video and packaging should be retained until the complaint is resolved.

C. 48-Hour Reporting Deadline

The customer should contact The Antique Rose within 48 hours of the recorded delivery date and time to report an eligible delivery issue. Requests raised after this period may be declined under the standard voluntary policy, where legally permissible. This deadline does not remove statutory consumer rights.

D. Return Request Workflow

1

Customer receives the parcel and checks the product.

2

Customer contacts support within 48 hours of recorded delivery.

3

Customer submits the continuous unboxing video through WhatsApp.

4

Support verifies the order ID, delivery timestamp, product details and evidence.

5

Support approves, declines or requests clarification, with reasons recorded.

6

If approved, support communicates collection, return and resolution instructions.

E. Standard Non-Eligible Voluntary Requests

  • Change-of-mind requests, where no legal return right applies
  • Preference-based requests for a different design or colour
  • Damage caused by use, mishandling or alteration after delivery
  • Products returned without required components or packaging where reasonably necessary for verification
  • Requests outside the standard 48-hour reporting period, where legally permissible

Replacement Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

ReturnsExchangeRefundsContact Support

The standard business resolution for an approved damaged, defective or incorrect product is replacement with the same product, subject to stock availability and applicable law.

Replacement Conditions

  • The issue must be reported within the standard 48-hour window, subject to legal exceptions.
  • A continuous unboxing video is the standard required evidence; photographs alone are not a substitute.
  • The same product, design and specification should be supplied where available.
  • The customer should retain the original item and packaging until collection instructions are provided.
  • If the identical product is unavailable, the available lawful resolution will be communicated rather than promising an unavailable replacement.

Replacement Workflow

1

Customer raises the issue and provides order details.

2

Customer submits the unboxing video.

3

Support reviews and records the decision.

4

If approved, support arranges collection or return instructions where required.

5

The same-product replacement is prepared and dispatched.

6

Customer receives dispatch confirmation and tracking details.

Exchange Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

ReturnsReplacementRefundsContact Support

Exchange is separate from replacement. Replacement addresses an approved product issue (same product returned); exchange usually means a customer-requested change to a different design, colour or product.

ReplacementExchange

The Antique Rose's standard position: routine preference-based exchanges or monetary exchanges are not offered. Same-product replacement is the standard remedy for verified eligible issues. Any restriction remains subject to applicable consumer law.

Refund Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

ReturnsReplacementCancellationContact Support

The Antique Rose's standard business position is replacement-focused, but this policy clearly explains when a refund is legally required or otherwise approved.

  • No routine cash refunds for change-of-mind requests where no refund right applies.
  • Approved eligible complaints will generally be resolved through same-product replacement where appropriate and lawful.
  • If the same product is unavailable, the available resolution will be communicated.
  • Where a refund is required or approved, it will be processed through the original payment method where applicable.
  • The customer will be informed when the refund has been initiated and provided a reference where available.
Please Note: The issue-reporting deadline, refund approval date and payment-provider crediting time are separate. The time taken for the refund to reflect in your account depends on your payment provider and is outside The Antique Rose's control.

Cancellation Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

ShippingRefundsReturnsContact Support

Before Dispatch

  • Customers may request cancellation before dispatch through official support channels.
  • We will verify the order ID and check the shipment status.
  • If the order has not been dispatched, eligible cancellation requests will be processed subject to applicable law.
  • If payment has already been made, any applicable refund will be handled under the Refund Policy.

After Dispatch

  • The normal cancellation process may no longer be available after dispatch.
  • Customers should contact support for assistance.
  • Any delivery refusal, return or refund request will be assessed under the relevant policy and applicable consumer rights.

Cancellation confirmation will include the order ID, cancellation date, status and refund status where applicable.
